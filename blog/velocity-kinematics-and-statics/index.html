Velocity Kinematics

We are interested in transformation from joint space to task space. Forward kinematics is about calculating the robot end-effector frame's position and orientation for agiven set of joint positions. $$ \theta \in \mathbb R^n \xrightarrow{\text{Forward Kinematics}} T(\theta) := \begin{bmatrix} R(\theta) & p(\theta) \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \in SE(3) $$ where $\theta \in \mathbb R^n$ is a set of joint angles, $R \in SO(3)$, and $p \in \mathbb R^3$. In this post, we will talk about other transformation form joint space to task space. It is the problem of calculating the twist of the end-effctor of an open chain from a given set of joint positions and velocities. $$ \dot \theta \in \mathbb R^n \xrightarrow[\mathcal V = \textcolor{red}{J(\theta)} \dot \theta]{\text{Velocity Kinematics}} \mathcal V := \begin{bmatrix} v \\ \omega \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb R^6 $$
