Overview

I worked as a member of the frontend development team for UOSLIFE, a community app for the University of Seoul. Our team developed and operated 시대팅, an on-campus matchmaking service that received over 1,700 applications. My role was to develop a matching algorithm that maximized user satisfaction and to build the web app frontend. I collaborated closely with the project planning team to discuss questions such as, "What information should we collect to provide ideal matches for both users, and is it technically feasible?" I also continuously coordinated with the design team to find a balance between user-friendly design and development feasibility. Based on these collaborations, I developed the frontend for the app.
